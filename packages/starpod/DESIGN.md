# Design System: Starpod

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Typeset Conversation"**

A podcast is people talking, so Starpod typesets the conversation. The episode stage puts the video (or the show's artwork) beside a live script in which each voice has its own face, the line being heard swells to display size, and spoken lines shrink and soften back into the page. Everything else (the list of episodes, the shell, the pages) speaks in the same condensed, uppercase display voice and a calm grotesque for UI.

One engine runs the whole visit. A single persistent `<video>` element plays the episode's HLS video or MP3, lives on the episode stage, in the dock or floating in a corner, flies between them, and writes playback state onto `<html data-*>` and its clock as `--live-*` custom properties (in steps of 1/400 on `<html>`, exact on the few elements that show it) so CSS can react without islands. Light and dark are both first-class: day uses Iris, night uses Aqua. The styles are still scoped under `<html data-world="stage">`, which is hardcoded; there is one world.

The system is generic by construction. Show artwork and cast photos supply the only imagery colour, shown in full colour, and every surface has to look deliberate for any show's art.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One OKLCH palette, one shell, one Stage world.
- Light is computed, not painted: the scrubber, the dock edge, and the stage light all take their position or colour from real playback.
- Iris by day, Aqua by night. The accent marks interaction, current state, and playback, nothing else.
- Three hover behaviours, never mixed: a gradient ring on outlined controls, a gradient fill on solid ones, a gradient icon on bare icons.
- Condensed uppercase Archivo for every title; Familjen Grotesk for everything you read or operate.
- Fixed navigation keeps play, follow, and navigation on screen at every size.

## Colors

Cool violet-grey neutrals carrying two rationed accents: Iris (violet) owns the light scheme and Aqua (cyan) owns the dark one. Tailwind exposes the live tokens as utilities (`text-ink`, `bg-surface`, `border-line`, `ring-accent-soft`, `from-glow-a` …) through `@theme inline`, so one class follows both schemes.

### Primary
- **Iris** (iris-500): the light-scheme accent. Focus rings, selection, caret, the buffering arc, and the bright end of the day gradient.
- **Deep Iris** (iris-700): light-scheme accent ink, for text in the accent: current nav items, seek chips, the "Video" kind marker, prose links. It is also voice 0's colour by day.
- **Iris Wash** (iris-100): the light-scheme soft accent fill behind current nav items and seek chips, and the focus ring around fields.
- **Pale Iris** (iris-200): the soft end of the day gradient. **Lilac** (iris-300) is voice 0 by night.

### Secondary
- **Aqua** (aqua-400): the dark-scheme accent, playing every role Iris plays by day.
- **Pale Aqua** (aqua-200): dark-scheme accent ink, the bright end of the night gradient, and voice 1 by night.
- **Deep Aqua** (aqua-700): voice 1's colour by day.
- **Night Accent Wash** (accent-soft-night): the dark-scheme soft accent fill.

### Neutral
- **Ground / Surface / Raised** (day and night): page ground, then the shell panels, cards, and fields, then hover fills and keyboard hints. All are faintly violet-tinted so neither scheme reads as dead grey.
- **Line / Line Strong**: hairline dividers and every default border (a bare `border` resolves to Line). Line Strong is for secondary-button and switch borders and the breadcrumb chevron.
- **Ink / Text / Muted**: headings and primary controls, then body, then metadata and secondary labels. **On Ink** is the text on ink-filled controls and flips with the scheme. **On Glow** is the text on the gradient fill (ink by day, on-ink by night).
- **Media Ground** (media-ground-day / -night): the near-black field behind video and artwork in the stage frame and mini player. **On-Media Chip / Ink** form the fixed light play disc over media, and **Media Glass** is the translucent dark "Resume at" chip beside it; all three ignore the scheme.

### Named Rules
**The Two Lamps Rule.** Iris is the only accent by day and Aqua the only accent by night. Accent fills are limited to interaction, the current item, and playback, and never decorate resting content.

**The Computed Light Rule.** Every moving gradient or glow takes its position from the media's published clock (`--live-progress` and friends), from sampled video, or from a hover/focus state. A gradient that no state drives does not belong in the system.

**The OKLCH Only Rule.** Every colour is authored in OKLCH, including shadows, masks, and backdrops. Hex and rgb are never used.

**The One Palette Rule.** Tailwind's theme holds only the token colours above. There are no parallel light/dark colour pairs; a single token class follows both schemes.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo variable, with weight and width axes (falls back to Familjen Grotesk)
**UI / Body Font:** Familjen Grotesk (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Voice 1:** Young Serif (with ui-serif, Georgia)

**Character:** Archivo, condensed to 72% width and set uppercase at 800, is the loud stage voice for every title. Familjen Grotesk is the calm voice for reading and operating. Young Serif answers Archivo as the second speaker in the script. All numerals are tabular.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, 72% width, uppercase, `display`): the episode title on the stage, max 18ch.
- **Page title** (800, 72% width, uppercase, `page-title`): the `PageHeader` on About, Contact, and other standalone pages, max 18ch, balanced. The 404 heading uses the same face one step smaller.
- **Rail title** (900, 62% width, `rail-title`): the show name in the desktop rail.
- **Headline** (800, 72% width, uppercase, `headline`): section titles ("Show notes", "Listen on", "Meet the hosts").
- **Row title** (800, 72% width, uppercase, `row-title`): episode titles in the list; the current episode's title takes the voice-0 colour.
- **Lede** (1.125rem/1.625, max 62ch, Text): the paragraph under a page title.
- **Body** (400, 1rem/1.55): UI text. Stage descriptions are 1.0625rem, max 62ch. Show notes and transcripts use `body-prose` via Tailwind `prose` (max ~70–76ch).
- **Label** (650, 0.75rem, 0.04em tracking): rail group labels ("Hosted by", "Follow the show"), "Presented by", Host/Guest tags (uppercase), and field labels at 0.875rem 600.
- **Script**: the live line (`data-d="0"`) sets at `script-live-*` in its voice's face. One and two lines back step down to 1.4rem and 1.15rem; three to five back drop to 0.9rem with a 0.4px blur; upcoming lines sit at 0.95rem. Long live lines cap at clamp(1.75rem, 1rem + 1.9vw, 2.75rem).

### Named Rules
**The One Face Per Voice Rule.** In the script and the cast, voice 0 is condensed Archivo in the voice-0 colour, voice 1 is Young Serif in the voice-1 colour and right-aligned, and further voices or unattributed captions are Familjen in ink. A voice face always identifies a speaker.

**The Volume Rule.** Size, width, and opacity carry distance from the live line. They are set by the engine's `data-d` attribute and never by hand.

**The Upright Time Rule.** Every timestamp is upright, tabular, and in `HH:MM:SS` on seek chips.

## Layout

Layout is an app shell with a single main column.

- **Desktop (≥1024px):** a sticky full-height rail (272px) holds the show art (with a tilt on hover), show name, blurb, search, navigation, follow links, and hosts. It collapses to a 72px icon rail (toggle at its top, `[`, or its divider), remembered across visits as `html[data-rail]`; `--rail-w` is its at-rest width where chrome outside the page reads it (never changed on `:root`, which would restyle the whole page), and the divider is the anchor `--rail-edge` for anything that should ride the rail live. The dock spans the remaining width at the bottom (84px). The main column pads by the dock height plus 64px and caps at 1280px on pages.
- **Below 1024px:** a sticky top bar (60px plus safe area) carries the show and an ink "Follow" pill; it lies flat on the page at the top and frosts over, with a hairline, once the page scrolls under it. A fixed three-up tab bar (Episodes, Search, More; 62px plus safe area) sits at the bottom with the dock (68px) above it. Sheets rise from the bottom as native popovers, max 560px wide, with the tray radius (`--r-tray`) and a 44px circular close in the top corner.
- **Gutter:** clamp(16px, 3.2vw, 48px). Breadcrumbs, page titles, and content share one left edge.
- **Home:** the latest episode opens the page in a 16:9 media frame (artwork on its ambient blur, a stage play disc) beside its title, its people (a row of 32px ringed circles with their names, each a link to the archive filtered to them), summary and a labelled play; then the archive explorer. Its toolbar sticks to the top (under the top bar below 1024px): search with the live count inside it, Grid/List, and Back to top once you are deep in the archive; below, a sideways-scrolling chip row with All/Video/Audio, In progress, Year, With (hosts and guests), Reset and Sort. Every control in the toolbar is 40px high. A filter with a value set turns solid ink and its chevron gives way to a small × that clears it; the With chip wears the chosen person's face (the people identity at 20px). Menus take `--r-card`, flip when there is no room, and list 44px rows at `--r-row`, the label left and a radio right. Filter, sort and view live in the URL query (`q`, `kind`, `guest`, `year`, `progress`, `sort`, `view`).
- **Episode stage (desktop):** the frame and meta sit on the left beside a sticky script column at 0.95fr. With no transcript the stage collapses to one column, max 1100px. On phones the script performs directly under the picture (max 34vh), then the meta.
- **Episode body:** under the stage, a sticky episode bar (under the top bar on phones, at the top beside the rail) carries the section links, then Notes, People, Presented by, Transcript and More episodes, each present only when the episode has it.
- **Standalone pages:** `PageHeader`, then sections separated by 4–5rem. About gives each host a poster, two across from 1024px: the photo on top, its lower third fading into a colour sampled from it (`img-color`), the name in the host's voice, "106 episodes · since 2021 · 128 hours", the bio, a tray of their two latest episodes with an "All 106" pill, and their links as pills with a glyph. When every host's tray would hold the same episodes, the posters keep only their pill and the episodes they made together show once beneath them, as Latest episodes. Contact keeps the form to 36rem with name and email side by side from 640px; each label takes an accent check once its field is valid, and the send button's lower edge fills with the form's completion (prop-for-that `field` / `form-state`). Long pages (For LLMs) reuse the episode bar's section links.
- **Picture-in-picture:** when a playing episode's stage scrolls away, its picture (the video, or the artwork while listening) flies to a corner of the room between the chrome: below the top bar's height, above the dock, beside the rail. It is clamp(260px, 24vw, 400px) wide on desktop and min(52vw, 360px) on phones, shrinking to fit a short room, and never covers the dock or changes the page's layout. Listening docks the artwork in the dock instead by default.
- **Foldables** (two viewport segments, or a coarse-pointer tablet aspect): the stage becomes two pages either side of a hinge gap of at least 40px; voice 0 takes the left page and voice 1 the right.
- **Breakpoints:** 640px, 768px, 1024px, 1100px.

**The Never-Hidden CTA Rule.** Play, follow, and navigation are fixed chrome at every size: the rail on desktop, the top bar plus tab bar plus dock below that. Page content pads for every fixed layer it could sit under.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes mainly from tone (Ground → Surface → Raised) and from light sampled out of the media. Shadows are reserved for objects that float above the page.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Soft** (`--shadow-soft`, two-layer violet-tinted by day, black by night): the stage frame, show art, and mini-player chrome.
- **Float** (`--shadow-float`): things lifted off the page, such as the artwork in the stage frame, the stage play disc, bottom sheets, the floating picture and the island (deeper in its card home).
- **Dock lip** (a single upward shadow, `0 -12px 32px -24px`): separates the dock from content scrolling under it.

### Named Rules
**The Stage Light Rule.** The glow behind the stage frame is sampled from the playing video into a 32×18 canvas (artwork glow when nothing plays). It is the only ambient colour on the page and it is always computed.

**The Two Shadows Rule.** Soft and Float are the only elevation shadows. A hover is shown by the ring, the gradient fill, or a Raised fill, never by a deeper shadow or a lift.

## Shapes

Interactive controls are pills or circles (999px): buttons, play discs, the medium switch, platform pills, search, and the Follow pill. Containers are near-square (3px, or 4px for large surfaces like the frame). Fields are softer (12px), show art and the mini player 12px, row and dock art 8px, seek chips 6px, sheets 22px at the top. People are circles everywhere (`people.css`). Components nest concentrically on the component scale, an inner radius being its container's minus the inset between them: entity cards, popovers and menus `--r-card` (28px); trays, sheets' panels and nested panels `--r-tray` (24px), 4px inside a card; art tiles and media `--r-tile` (20px); rows in panels `--r-row` (16px); small thumbs and hit areas `--r-thumb` (12px). The hairline, `--hairline` (Ink at 10%), sits over every image and portrait as an inset 1px ring above the picture. Colour and fill changes on small controls take `--quick` (150ms). Iconography is one 24px, 1.75-stroke outline set (`icons.tsx`), with filled play and pause glyphs and skip icons that print their seconds inside.

**The Pill Controls Rule.** If it can be pressed, it is round. Corner radius on containers is near-square; corner radius on controls is full.

**The Shape-Keeping Focus Rule.** Focus never changes an element's shape. The global ring is a 2px accent outline at 3px offset that follows the element's own radius; fields replace it with an accent border and a 4px Accent Wash ring.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 0.75rem × 1.25rem padding, 0.9375rem at 700.
- **Primary (solid):** ink fill with On Ink text ("Play episode", "Send message"). Hover and focus fade the fill into the Glow A→B gradient over 300ms (registered `--fill-a/--fill-b`) and the text to On Glow. Press scales to 0.97 on the spring.
- **Secondary (outlined):** surface fill, 1px Line Strong border, ink text ("Read the full transcript", "Go back"). Hover and focus draw the ring.
- **Icon button:** a 40px circle with no fill at rest. Hover turns the icon itself into the gradient (each SVG carries its own `linearGradient`, stops transition 300ms).
- **Text button:** a 34px pill with a Line border, used for the playback rate; wears the ring.

### Play discs
- **Stage play:** clamp(56px, 6vw, 76px) circle with the fixed light On-Media disc and Float shadow, bottom-left on the frame. It fades out while a video plays and returns on frame hover or focus. A Media Glass "Resume at" chip sits beside it.
- **Dock play:** 44px ink circle; hover fills with the gradient. Press scales it to 0.92.
- **Row play:** 44px surface circle with a Line Strong border and the ring on hover. On the current episode it turns into a solid ink disc with three on-air bars that pulse while playing and give way to the glyph on hover.
- **Buffering:** a 2px accent arc spins around the disc (`.play-toggle[data-buffering]::before`, 900ms linear).
- **Glyph:** play and pause spring between each other.

### Medium switch (Watch / Listen)
A pill track on Ground with a Line Strong border and 3px inset. An ink thumb slides under the chosen option on the spring (520ms) and the chosen label turns On Ink. Wears the ring. A compact size is used in the dock.

### Inputs
- **Style:** 1px Line border, Surface fill, 12px radius, 0.75rem × 1rem padding, with a visible label above (0.875rem 600 ink). Placeholders are Muted and only add an example.
- **Focus:** the border turns Accent and a 4px Accent Wash ring appears (200ms); the outline is removed so the field keeps its radius.
- **States:** the submit button disables and reads "Sending…"; errors appear as a bordered note above the button with `role="alert"`; success replaces the form with a Surface panel.
- **Search palette:** a Surface panel (`--r-card`) with a Line outline that grows out of the search trigger on screen and flies back into it; the field has no ring of its own, and the hairline under it turns Accent while focused. Before typing it offers the people most on the show (no count yet); a query that names someone puts up to three person rows (44px, a 32px ringed circle, an episode-count chip) above the episodes, and choosing one opens the archive filtered to them (`/#guest=<id>`). What was typed stays plain and the completion is bold. Rows sit 8px in, at the panel's radius less 8. Results carry their art; the selection is a Raised highlight that glides between rows.

### Navigation
Rail and sheet links are 600 weight with a 24px muted icon; in the rail they are pills, in the icon rail 48px circles on a 56px pitch, and in a phone sheet 48px rows in a nested panel (the sheet's tray radius, rows at it less 4) with the icon in a 32px circle. Hover gives a Raised fill and ink text. The current page gets the Accent Wash fill and accent-ink text and icon. In the rail that fill is one highlight that glides to the hovered or focused link, or else rests on the current page, and gives a little under a press. Config `links` choose an icon (`external`, `heart`, `link`, `sponsor`, `store`; default `external` for URLs, `link` for local pages). The tab bar is three even cells with a 0.6875rem label; the current cell is accent ink.

### Breadcrumbs
0.8125rem Muted links with a Line Strong chevron, an even 8px either side; the current crumb is Text at 500. Hover turns a crumb Ink. Hidden on the episode stage.

### Episode cards
The archive shows each episode as a card in a grid (2 to 6 columns by the explorer's width) or as a hairline-ruled list row. A card is square full-colour art (`--r-tile` under the hairline; `--r-thumb` in the list and the filmstrip) with Media Glass chips for its kind and length and a progress light along its bottom edge for episodes the visitor has started, the row play disc on the art's corner (at the row's end in the list), then, 8px under the art and 4px in from its edge, the facts line in sentence case (Muted 0.8125rem 500, tabular, the number in ink: "Ep 260 · Nov 12, 2026", length and kind joining in the list and the close-up; a separator never starts a line) and the condensed uppercase title (three lines in the grid, two in the list, where the description joins from 40rem). Every card's size is exact (square art plus a fixed text block, from the stage's width), so a card rendering in never re-lays the archive. Under the pointer a card's art grows (a flat scale from its centre, 240ms ease, both ways): a tile to 1.06, a list row's thumb to 1.12 while the row takes a Raised fill; no card in the wall is ever in 3D. The title is the link to the episode; the art, or anywhere else on a card, opens it in the close-up.

Every change to the archive moves the cards on screen with PropFLIP, as three boxes: the art scales (it is square in every layout, so it never squishes), the text block only travels and fades its re-wrapped lines in where it lands, and the play disc travels. Cards cascade 16ms apart in the order shown; cards that leave fade where they stood; cards that join rise in with `@starting-style`.

### Signature: the close-up
Opening an episode is the Close-Up (propflip-anchored-auto-layout's `demos/closeup.html`): one card, three destinations, some depth. The card's artwork, facts line and title are loose parts; each re-targets to its own anchored slot in a close-up layout, on three delays (0, 60, 120ms; the play disc follows at 180ms into the detail's row). The title grows as type (its font size tweens, it never scales) and tumbles one turn; the artwork turns over in 3D one level below the box that travels, to a back with the episode number and kind printed on the picture, sized from the card's own width so the card flies home as the open card made smaller. In the same change the archive folds into a filmstrip beside the close-up (along the bottom on phones) that leans back in space, seen from the middle of the page; the page itself scrolls the strip and the close-up sticks under the toolbar, so nothing in flight is ever clipped. The detail column rises in line by line: description, cast, where to resume, play, Add to Up next, Open episode. The arrow keys step through the results as shown (filtered and sorted), and a step mid-flight sends the old parts home as the new ones leave; Escape closes and returns focus to the artwork; a filter that drops the open episode closes it. A soft glow from the artwork's own colour (`img-color`) sits behind it. The picture leads: it takes whatever a 17 to 26rem column of text leaves it, as tall as the close-up allows; where the stage is narrower than 56rem (a tablet, a desktop with the sidebar open, a phone) the facts and title sit beside it and the detail beneath. The cast is the people identity: 32px ringed circles with each name and what this episode is for them ("Host · 106 episodes", "First time on the show"). Opening the episode from the close-up morphs its turned artwork and title into the episode page. Without anchor positioning the card opens in place instead, the full width of the archive.

### Live state and stagger
Cards and the hero read live state from prop-for-that rather than script: the playing episode's progress light follows `--live-progress` and the hero's on-air levels stop with `--live-paused`; a light sweeps across a thumbnail still loading as it scrolls into view (a `view()` timeline, gated on `--live-loaded`); the hero frame lights the rim nearest the cursor with the house ring gradient; the hero's artwork leans toward the pointer and casts its own colour (`img-color`) away from it; list rows show "More" only while `truncated` says the description is cut off. A sort sets `order` with `reading-flow: grid-order` instead of moving cards in the DOM.

Siblings take their stagger from the tree (`sibling-index()`): menu choices arrive one after another, chips that come and go arrive in the row's order, the close-up's detail column and its cast rise line by line, and the on-air levels take their tempo and phase from their place. The archive's own cascade is the one exception: a card's place in the DOM is not its place on screen (sorting sets `order`, filters hide cards), so the cascade runs over the cards on screen in the order shown. Off screen, nothing runs: cards skip rendering (`content-visibility`), the strip's vantage is a `view()` timeline, and the hero's levels rest when they are out of view. Off screen, cards are not even there: only the cards around the viewport (a screen either way) are boxes in the archive's grid. The rest wait in two parked blocks before and after them, each one box spanning exactly the rows its cards would take (every row is one height), laying its cards on the grid's own tracks (subgrid) and hidden until found, so find in page opens a block where the match is and the window moves there. The window follows the scroll; a change re-windows around the card that holds the reader's place, the close-up around its card. A card on screen whose new place falls outside the window fades out where it stood, as a filtered card does; cards that join a cut fade up (`@starting-style`) only when they really join. The page still carries every episode, and every row keeps its place, so nothing on screen moves when cards come and go.

### Platform pills
Labelled pills (About, the follow sheet): 1px Line border, an 18px platform glyph in Muted, ink label, the ring on hover. In the rail they are 40px icon-only circles on Ground. Wherever they wrap they sit in balanced rows, worked out per item from `sibling-index()` and `sibling-count()`: no row more than one shorter than another, the longer rows first, never a lone mark when a row can take one more (six make 3 + 3, five 3 + 2, seven 4 + 3). In the follow sheet each row fills the width, two to a row on phones and three from 560px.

### Seek chips
Timestamps in show notes and transcripts are 6px-radius chips in Accent Wash with accent-ink 600 tabular `HH:MM:SS`. Hover and focus fill them with the gradient. Brackets around a chapter time are absorbed into the chip. Clicking seeks the player.

### Cast and sponsors
The stage lists the episode's hosts (configured order, then guest hosts) and guests (alphabetical) as 52px ringed portraits (36px on phones), names in their voice's face when they speak as voice 0 or 1, and a sentence-case "Host"/"Guest". "Presented by" lists the episode's sponsors, each logo on a 40px white circle, and hides when empty.

### People identity
One portrait everywhere a person appears (`people.css`, `PersonFace.astro`): a circle in a 2px ring of their voice (Voice 0 or Voice 1 when they speak as one, Line Strong otherwise), 2px clear of the photo, with the hairline over it. While their line is live (wherever they appear, the transcript's speaker card included) the ring brightens to the accent with a soft halo, by opacity alone; under reduced motion a small "Speaking" label shows instead. The line under a name says something, the first that applies (`personFact`): "First time on the show", "3rd appearance · first on Ep 112", "Host · 106 episodes"; away from an episode, role and tally. Compact rows are 32px portraits with the name at 14/18 600.

### Episode bar
A 56px bar below the stage that sticks once it reaches the top, taking the top bar's blurred glass while stuck. Section links are muted 650 pills; the current section is tracked by the browser (`scroll-target-group`) and marked by one Accent Wash pill anchored to that link, which glides between links on the spring. Once the stage's meta has scrolled away, a scroll trigger grows the episode's ink play disc and title in from the left, the disc, number and name arriving in that order. While this episode is the one loaded, the disc's ring fills with `--live-progress` from the media clock bound on that ring alone (`data-props-for="media-clock"`), in the accent while playing and Muted while paused (`--live-paused`), pushing the links over; scrolling back reverses it. A 2px Line Strong rule along the lower edge fills with reading progress. When the links overflow, their edges fade only on the side with more.

### Transcript turns
Each paragraph that opens with a timestamp is a turn: a 7rem gutter (5.5rem for feed transcripts, which carry no speakers) holds the seek chip and the speaker's name in their voice's face. A long turn's head is sticky while the turn is on screen, trailing a fading rule down the rest of the turn once stuck. The turn being heard takes the Accent Wash with an accent chip. A "Now" ink pill sticks to whichever screen edge the live turn is past, only once all of it is off screen, and points to it. Long transcripts come in groups of 20 turns that skip rendering off screen, which keeps them at the display's frame rate while playing; an unrendered group holds the first group's measured height, and a section link that glides past groups re-aims once they have rendered. Hovering a name (or focusing a chip) shows the person on a Float card above it, or below it when the name is stuck under the bar.

### Stage split
On desktop, when the episode has a transcript, one 1px Line divider between the picture with its meta and the live script is the only anchor; both sides are anchored to it, so where it sits is the whole layout (`episode/split.ts`, after anchored-auto-layout's split demo). Watch, Balanced and Read (the picture's side at 66%, 52% and 36%) sit on a pill segmented control above the script with one ink indicator gliding to the pressed one. Dragging writes the divider's position and nothing animates; letting go projects the throw and glides to the stop it was heading for, and catching it mid-glide holds it where it is. Double-click swaps Watch and Read; arrow keys, Home and End step through the stops. The picture resizes with its side, the title sets to the side's width, and the script re-forms by its own width: the full exchange, then a compact one, then only the line being heard. The choice is remembered on the device. Phones, browsers without typed `attr()`, and episodes without a transcript keep the grid stage.

### People, sponsors and onward
Who leads depends on the episode: with guests, the guests get cards and the hosts follow on one compact "Hosted by" line, each linking to the explorer filtered by them; with none, the hosts get the cards. A card is Surface at 28px, barely floating: the 64px portrait, the name at 17/22 650 on one line (two on phones), the fact line, and a trailing stat, a "First speaks" seek chip to their first line when the transcript names them, otherwise a quiet count chip ("3 eps"). With a transcript, a 4px bar in their voice along the card's lower edge shows their share of the talking. Only a guest with other episodes opens: in place across the full row, its neighbours flipping into the gaps, to a Raised tray 4px in holding up to four square art tiles (a glass "Ep 240" chip on each, the title beneath in two lines), then an "All N episodes with X" pill; on phones the tray scrolls sideways and the next tile peeks. Hover tints the card; nothing lifts. Sponsors are outlined 28px cards: the logo on a white circle, the name at 15/20 650, "Presented 8 episodes", and a trailing "Visit" pill. More episodes pairs Previous and Next cards (28px, art 20px 8px in) with a snap carousel of related episodes (the guests' other episodes first, each saying why with a 20px portrait, then neighbours in time); the next card always peeks, the card at rest leads at full size and the rest sit back slightly, and the arrow that can't go further is gone rather than dimmed. Meta under titles is sentence case, and every piece of art carries the hairline. Cards and section titles rise in on a view timeline over the first 120px of their entry, siblings a little further into it one after another (`sibling-index()`), so a row arrives as a wave; related cards, in their sideways shelf, fade and scale in on the shelf's own view timeline the same way. Opening a person ripples outward from the card you touched: its neighbours set off by their distance from it, and the panel's episodes follow one after another. People, sponsor and previous/next cards carry a soft accent glow that follows the pointer (`pointer-local`), the ring an outlined card draws on hover turns its brightest side toward the pointer, and related-episode art leans toward it. Art and portraits sit on a still Raised placeholder until the image loads.

### Previews
Long show notes (34rem) and transcripts (26rem) open as a preview faded out in fixed lengths, so shorter content is never faded. Their outlined "more" button sits over the fade, and only appears when prop-for-that's `truncated` reports the preview really clips.

### The island, the picture and Up next
The player is one island with four homes, after the Island demo: a **pill** (art, title, on-air bars, play) low and central in the page column, clear of the sticky bars at the top of main; the **dock**, a full transport bar inset 8px (14px on desktop) above the bottom edge; the **card**, the large now-playing surface beside the rail (full height on phones) over a dimmed page; and the **rail**, inside the sidebar (a vertical stack of art, bars, back, play and forward in the icon rail; a compact card in the open rail; not offered on phones). The visitor chooses with a segmented control whose one ink indicator glides to the checked home (in the card, and in a dock wide enough for it), with the keys 1 to 4, or by clicking the island's body, which opens the card and closes it back to the home it came from (Escape too). The home persists and is published as `<html data-island>`.

From 1024px the dock re-forms by its own width (its station's, which does not tween, so a journey flies straight to its final form, and a sidebar folding under it flips the parts to their new places). The scrubber is the last thing it sheds: it always keeps a track of 240px or more and takes the free space, and the picture, title, transport and play always stay. As the dock narrows, the home control folds into one "Move player" button whose popover holds the same choice; the watch/listen switch shows its icons only; speed and mute move into the popover (the button becomes "Player options"); then the switch, the picture button and the bars; and at the narrowest the times and Play next, the title giving way to 140px and then 96px. Nothing a width hides is out of reach: it is in that popover, which opens above its button (below where there is no room, its caret turning with it through an anchored container query). On phones the dock keeps art, title, play and next, and the card holds the rest.

The homes are empty stations; the island fills one (`data-⚓️-fill` + `data-⚓️-to`) and its own left, top, width and height tween to the next, x on a soft spring (0.7s), y on a livelier one (0.8s), size on the soft one (0.6s), so the glass, radius and clipping never squish; at rest it sits on its station exactly, so it rides the sidebar's divider held or gliding. Its grid re-templates per home and every part is a PropFLIP leaf that inverts against the island and rides home on an 18ms cascade from its place in the tree (`sibling-index()`; from the far end on the way back). Parts a home does not use park behind a neighbour and fade, so they fly out of it and back into it; nothing unmounts. The island takes the radius scale and nests it: the card at `--r-card`, the dock and the rail at `--r-tray`, the pill and the icon rail fully round; the art at `--r-tile` in the card, `--r-thumb` in the dock and open rail, a circle in the pill and icon rail, always under a hairline. The line under the title is sentence case ("Ep 260 · Watching"). The glass is Surface at 80% with a 28px blur, tinted 16% by the artwork's accent, which tweens over 1.2s when the episode changes. The on-air bars run only while it plays. The scrubber is a 4px track filled with the gradient to the playhead (painted from the published clock, not re-rendered), a 14px ink thumb, tabular times, and a transcript preview of the line at the hovered time; on phones the dock shows a 2px light under its title instead, dimmed while paused. The clock is bound only on the parts that show it (the scrubber, the title and the picture's bar), and its lights step with it rather than tweening between ticks, so while something plays a tick restyles a handful of elements and nothing in the player runs every frame.

The picture (the video, or the artwork standing in for it) is the island's art part when it is docked or the card is open, and otherwise has its own homes: an episode's stage and a corner of the screen. Every journey is a PropFLIP from the pixels it is on into a box anchored to where it is going, with its size tweening for real and an ease-out across against a slight settle down (0.52s / 0.66s), so it arcs and can turn around mid-flight. The stage hands it over on a scroll-triggered animation; navigations carry it live across the page change. Floating, it sits on Media Ground with the Float shadow, a glow in the colour of what is playing, a slight lean toward the pointer and a soft sheen that follows it, under a Surface bar (title, move to the next corner, open the card, fullscreen, close, and a 2px progress light along its foot) whose caret points at the picture and whose controls arrive in tree order. Its room ends above the island wherever the island sits at the bottom. Drag the picture and let go: it is tossed to the nearest corner, or into the island; the corner is remembered. Tapping it opens its episode. Close pauses and docks it; any play brings it back.

**Up next** lives in the card, after the Queue demo: now playing (a square in the middle of the art), Up next (a column of 56px slots with their titles, and one dashed slot to drop into) and a shelf of more episodes (fetched from the JSON API on idle once something has actually played, or only when the card first opens if the visitor asks to save data) are plain slots in a subgrid of the card. The board is drawn ahead of time while the card is parked and kept current on every change, so opening the card only flips; a shelf that arrives with the card open waits for the island to land and fades its covers in one after another. Covers are siblings that never change parent; each fills a slot by name (the tile on its anchor keeps still, and its face is what flies and is dragged, since Chrome lays an anchor-positioned box out again on any change to its style), and every change (a tap, a drag, Next, an episode ending, the explorer adding one) rewrites the names and flies every cover that moved in one 22ms-staggered flip, resizing between the big slot and the small ones. A cover landing in now playing gives way to the picture. Tap a shelf cover to play it next; tap a queued one to play it now; drag any cover onto any slot (held mid-flight, released with a forced flip). Keys: arrows move between covers, Alt with an arrow reorders Up next, Delete takes one out. Up next and the shelf are trays (`--r-tray`) inside the card; covers are art at `--r-tile` (the small Up next ones at `--r-thumb`) under a hairline, carry their number as a glass chip, and have a sentence-case caption beneath them or beside them ("Ep 240 · 1 hr 5 min"), 8px under the art and 4px in. A queued cover's remove control is a glass circle that shows only on hover or focus within. Without typed `attr()` (WebKit) the covers line up in slot order and every action still works, without the travel. Covers off screen cost nothing (`content-visibility: auto`), and the board costs nothing at all outside the card. Keyboard everywhere: K/space play, J/L skip, M mute, W watch/listen, F fullscreen, 1 to 4 the island's homes, Esc closes the card.

**The Now-Playing Colour.** The player takes one colour from what is playing: the live picture's most vivid lasting colour while watching (sampled once a second, off the main thread, eased and stepped, published on the glow alone), the artwork's accent while listening. It tints the floating picture's glow and the island's glass. It is always a tint over Surface or a shadow, never a surface itself, so it starts transparent and degrades to nothing. The player's outlined controls sweep their gradient ring on from the side the pointer comes in.

### Signature: the ring
A 1px gradient ring (Glow A to Glow B, to bottom right) that follows the element's own radius. On hover (pointer devices only) or keyboard focus it sweeps itself on over 500ms along `--ease-halo`, an animated `@property --halo` with `inherits: true`. It belongs only on outlined controls sitting on the page background.

**The Three Hovers Rule.** Outlined controls draw the ring; solid controls fill with the gradient; bare icons turn the icon to the gradient. A control gets exactly one, and every one transitions.

### Signature: the live script
The script column bottom-aligns its lines under a top fade mask and performs the conversation by distance. When the live line changes, each line FLIPs from its old box to its new one with transforms only (720ms, `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`), arrivals rise from below, and a jump of more than three lines crossfades instead. Hovering or focusing a line reveals its speaker and time; clicking seeks. It follows seeks and skips immediately.

### Signature: the rail
The rail is laid out twice: in-flow slots sized for its state, and the pieces you see anchored to them (Anchored Auto Layout), so collapsing is a choreography rather than a squeeze. The art shrinks to a 40px tile, the toggle arcs from the top corner to beneath it, the search pill and links narrow to 40px icons while their labels leave at once (and return only once there is room), the platform marks gather into a deck and the hosts into a column of overlapping faces. x eases out, y springs, each group travels up or down as one while cascading across and in size, and a second toggle reverses from wherever everything is. The rail is held by its divider (a focusable `separator`, 1px Line with a 5px handle on hover, focus and grab): dragging writes one number, the divider's x, and the rail and the page column follow it with nothing animating (a page that takes longer than a frame to re-lay rides on a translate while held and re-lays once when let go); the rail re-forms when the hand crosses the middle (172px), so it changes shape mid-drag and back again; between crossings its pieces sit in their slots, so a held frame costs the rail about a millisecond. Letting go projects the throw (the last 100ms of movement, coasting 220ms) to the nearer stop, PropFLIPs only the divider there, and the rail rides it while the page re-lays once and glides with the boxes other areas register for `flipLayout()`. It can be caught mid-glide; double-click and Enter toggle; arrows, Home and End step; the toggle and `[` take the same glide. Between the stops the title and blurb shrink with the rail rather than being cut by its edge. The now-playing island has a slot in the rail's flow (`--home-rail`), and the pieces below it make room as it grows. Hosted by reads as a co-author credit at rest, the faces overlapped in a tight stack (the shared ringed face, the show's first two hosts in their voice colours, the gap filled with Surface so overlapping faces part; a host's ring lights while they speak on whatever is playing, on any page, and under reduced motion a Speaking label follows their name instead) beside one line naming them together ("A & B"; "A, B and N more"); hovered, focused within or tapped it fans into a row per host, each face travelling to its own row, names arriving once the faces land, first out last back. The rows' room is kept below the stack, so nothing moves. One host is simply that host. Collapsed, one Ink pill tooltip travels between the icons on its anchor (open, it names only a link whose label is clipped), and each stack opens a Surface flyout (`--r-card`, 44px list rows with a 32px leading circle) that flips up near the bottom of the screen, its caret following (an anchored container query). While an episode is loaded the show art wears its progress as an Accent ring, dim when paused, read from the media clock it opts into (`data-props-for="media-clock"`); the art leans toward the pointer (CSS on pointer-local, styles/tilt.css); a soft Glow A light follows the pointer across the rail.

### Signature: page transitions
Navigations play a curtain: the old page drops away (240ms) and the new one rises in (640ms ease-out, slight blur), reversed on back. The rail, top bar, and tab bar cross-fade in 180ms, unpaired elements fade solo, and the dock never animates. The picture holds its own layer above the curtain and keeps flying through it.

### Signature, foldables: the unfold
On first detecting the spread, each page swings open once from the hinge (rotateY 62deg to 0, 1000ms expo-out), keyed by a one-shot `data-unfolding` attribute.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read state from the engine: style `[data-current]`, `[data-playing]`, `[data-watching]`, `[data-buffering]`, `html[data-medium]`, `html[data-host]`, and `--live-progress`/`--live-paused` (add `data-props-for="media-clock"` to an element for exact values on it) instead of adding new JS-driven classes.
- **Do** style new work with Tailwind utilities on the token colours; keep custom CSS for what Tailwind cannot express (`@property` animations, the ring, view transitions, voice typography).
- **Do** choose the hover by the control's shape: ring for outlined, gradient fill for solid, gradient icon for bare icons, always with a transition.
- **Do** use the easings: expo-out (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) for state and reveal, the `--ease-halo` curve for the ring, and the overshooting `linear()` spring for press, thumb, and glyph motion. Everything collapses to 1ms under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** open standalone pages with `PageHeader` and align them to the breadcrumbs.
- **Do** give fields visible labels and the accent border plus Accent Wash ring on focus.

### Don't:
- **Don't** author colour in hex, rgb, or hsl anywhere, including docs, shadows, and masks.
- **Don't** reintroduce parallel light/dark Tailwind colours (`bg-light-*`, `dark:bg-dark-*`). Use the single token class.
- **Don't** let an accent fill decorate resting content.
- **Don't** reshape an element on focus or put a ring on a control that already fills or has no outline.
- **Don't** add a third elevation shadow or a hover lift.
- **Don't** desaturate or duotone show art or cast photos; show imagery in full colour.
- **Don't** use a voice face (Archivo condensed as a speaker, Young Serif) for anything other than a speaker or their name; Archivo as a title face is fine.
- **Don't** hide play, follow, or navigation behind scroll.
- **Don't** add show-specific copy, colour, or imagery to the package. Show artwork and the site's own data are the only imagery and content sources.
